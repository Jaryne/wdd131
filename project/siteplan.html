NAME

Spider-Man 2099 Archives

I chose this name to let visitors know that the website is about Spider-Man 2099, also known as Miguel O'Hara from the Marvel Spider-Verse franchise. “Spider-Man 2099” identifies Miguel as the main subject, while “Archives” represents the collection of information about his identity, history, abilities, and media appearances. There is no domain name yet, but if the website were to have one, it would be 2099-archives.com so it would be easy for visitors to associate it with the character and website.

LOGO

PURPOSE

The purpose of the Miguel O'Hara Archives is to provide an interactive digital archive dedicated to Miguel O'Hara, also known as Spider-Man 2099. The site will present information about his identity, origin, abilities, relationship with Alchemax, and role within the Spider-Society, as well as his appearances in comics and films. Visitors will be able to explore the archive through an interactive futuristic interface and submit information through an archive submission form. The design is inspired by the cyberpunk setting of Spider-Man 2099 with my spin on the aesthetics, and uses interactive elements to make the character's information more engaging.

SCENARIOS

- Who is Miguel O'Hara, and how did he become Spider-Man 2099?
- What abilities and characteristics make Miguel O'Hara different from other Spider-Man characters?
- What is Miguel's connection to Alchemax and the Spider-Society?
- Where can I learn more about Miguel O'Hara's appearances in comics and films?
