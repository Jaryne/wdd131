Subject

A character-focused informational website about a fictional character.

Content

The website will provide information about the selected character, including their background, personality, notable traits, and other interesting details. It will also include images and a few sections highlighting different aspects of the character.

Reason

I chose this subject because I have always wanted to make a website about characters I like and share it with my family and friends. I see this project as an opportunity to dedicate time to creating something like that while also practicing the web development skills I have learned in this course.
